# Gut Waldegg: story bible (SPOILERS, developers only)

Files: `documents.ts` (interface + `DOCUMENTS`, 32 docs assembled from `docs_*.ts`), `environment_text.ts` (`ENV_TEXT`), `index.ts`.

## The truth (never stated in-game)

- **14 Nov 1987, ~15:50:** Marie Lindner (b. **9 Mar 1979**) walks into the fog to feed her rabbit Stupsi. Josef, splitting wood, is the last to see her.
- **That night** Josef searches the stream ravine alone with a lantern. The page for that night is torn out of his logbook. Two word fragments survive at the fold: "…arm" (warm? Arm?) and "…Gott". He says he found her in the leaves of the ravine, injured and alive. Next morning the dog trail ends at the water. The red mitten lies on a flat stone, "barely damp". Was it placed there? Never answered.
- Josef thinks God gave her back to him and that Friedrich (drunk, violent) does not deserve her. He hides her. From **26–28 Nov 1987** he lights the old coal-cellar boiler, carries down a mattress and buys iodine in Freistadt, "not Liebenau". She stays mute for months.
- Helga hears humming under the house. She sees a new pencil mark "M 1988" on the door frame and notices missing jumpers. Josef tells her it's a marten. The doctors call it grief. She is committed on **12 Mar 1991**.
- **11 Jun 1991:** Josef walls up the opening between the boiler cellar and the coal cellar before the court surveyor comes. The shelf of preserving jars goes in front of it. After that Marie may come up by day.
- **1993:** the power is cut. Josef's generator runs because "M. sleeps better". He raises her on a 1989 third-grade reader. He keeps **14 November as "Findetag" (Finding Day)**.
- **2003:** Josef is dying. He teaches her the generator, the money tin, ordering diesel by letter, the chapel key and "where the shovel stands". He has his own Parte printed. She fills in "JENNER 2004" and buries him under three spruces by the cemetery with no name, only his hat.
- **Afterwards:** diesel is still ordered as "Hrubý J." (2017) and she keeps the 2019 parish calendar. She lights candles for "J." and keeps Findetag (year 32). She broke the wall **from the inside**. She may still live here. **Never show her.** No figure, no silhouette. Show only warmth: fresh soot, a jar dated 2018, a stopped generator that is still warm.

**Czech lines (log 2001–03, untranslated in-game on purpose):** *Nevzal jsem ji. Našel jsem ji. Pán Bůh mi ji vrátil.* = "I did not take her. I found her. The Lord God gave her back to me." *Pane Bože, odpusť mi.* = "Lord God, forgive me."

## Clue chain (the "clicks")

1. **Birthday:** poster "geb. 9.3.1979" ↔ logbook "9.3. M. 9 Jahre" (1988), "M. 18" (1997), "M. 24" (2003).
2. **Height marks:** kitchen jamb (`ENV_TEXT.heightMarks`). Family marks are blue ballpoint and end at "Marie 1987, 127 cm". Then come "M 1988…M 1999" in carpenter's pencil, Josef's hand. Helga's letter to Fritz describes the first one.
3. **Coal cellar:** log 1987 (boiler, mattress, iodine) ↔ Helga to Gerti (humming "from below") ↔ letter to Fritz ("where the old coal cellar is"; traps, "never anything in them") ↔ log 1988 (marten lie, traps set "so she sees them") ↔ clinic: "unlock the cellar door at night and listen".
4. **Growing girl:** receipts 1987→1999 run size 128 → 140 → 152 → women's 38/40, plus sanitary towels in 1993, "not in Liebenau" and "for my niece". The schoolbooks receipt (1989, third grade, "from the beginning") ↔ log 1997 "reader for the fourth time" ↔ note_mama "that I know the whole reader".
5. **The wall:** log 1991 ("walled up, shelf in front") ↔ the boiler-cellar wall today: bricks and the toppled shelf lie on the boiler side, so it was pushed out from within. Tally in the sealed room: 1296 marks, the exact days 26 Nov 1987 → 14 Jun 1991.
6. **Moon song** ("Der Mond ist aufgegangen", verse 1 ends "der weiße Nebel wunderbar"): school page (Mama sings it) → Helga hears it stop just before the fog line → log 1997 she sings every verse → note_mama "up to the fog". The 1995 drawing has "no moon". The 2001 drawing has a huge moon (first night outside).
7. **Man with a hat / Stupsi:** the 1987 school drawing, 1995, 2001, the chapel drawing (a hat on a cross) and the real hat on the nameless cross. Stupsi appears on the kitchen blackboard, the toy box ("STUPSI WOHNT HIER") and the tally words.
8. **Josef is dead, yet:** Parte (unsent, mourners blank, "JENNER 2004" in the round hand) ↔ no named grave, but a nameless cross ↔ chapel drawing "J. SCHLAFT" ↔ calendar "candles J.". Yet the 2017 delivery note (ordered by letter, cash, chimney smoke) ↔ log 2003 lessons ↔ generator label copied in a second hand ↔ jars 2009–2018 ↔ calendar "FINDETAG. 32".
9. **Motive:** log 1986 (Marie flees across the yard at night) ↔ Gendarmerie statement ("I would rather not say"; he once "found her" at the hunting stand) ↔ Gerti letter (Fritz's hand) ↔ Friedrich's own note.

## Placement and discovery order

**Solo path:**
1. **Arrival:** delivery note (mailbox). In the caretaker's house: log 1986, clinic letter, half-burnt 1997 paper, 2019 calendar.
2. **Manor:** poster and Friedrich's note (hall), OÖN (salon), Gerti letter (dining), statement and bank (study). Height marks and blackboard in the kitchen, jars in the pantry. Then upstairs: school page and Helga's note (Marie's room, plus the unfaded floor patch where the toy box stood), Fritz letter and Bote (bedroom), prayer card (Oma), photo (Thomas), empty "Kleider 128–134" box (attic).
3. **Grounds:** log 1987 and receipts (workshop), log 1993 (pump house, generator), search card (hunting stand), seed packet (greenhouse).
4. **Underground:** log 1991 (boiler cellar, broken wall), schoolbooks receipt (storage), log 1988 and 2001 drawing (tunnel). In the sealed room: tally, toy box, 1995 drawing, note_mama.
5. **Return:** log 1997 (caretaker cellar), log 2001–03 and Parte (caretaker bedroom), nameless cross (cemetery), chapel drawing and fresh candles.

**Groups who split up:** the logbook is deliberately torn across three zones, so no team can read it alone.

| Team | Holds | Missing piece it needs |
|---|---|---|
| Manor | poster (9.3. / 14.11.), Helga's letters, height marks | who "M." is, why "Findetag" |
| Caretaker + workshop | logs 1986, 1987, 1997, 2001–03, receipts, Parte, calendar | the birthday, the coal cellar, the wall |
| Underground + pump house | logs 1988, 1991, 1993, drawings, note_mama, tally | the family context, Josef's fate |

The strongest shared moments are: 9.3 (poster + any log), 14.11 Findetag (poster + log 1997 + calendar), "M 1988" (kitchen + Fritz letter + workshop pencil) and the Parte vs. the 2017 delivery note.

## Conventions

- Austrian German. Anything written before 1998 uses pre-reform spelling (daß, vermißt, naß, "Samstag nachmittag"). Marie's adult notes still use "daß": she learned from 1989 books.
- Schilling until 2001, Euro from 2002. Weekdays in all dated entries are calendar-correct.
- Recurring minor names: Dr. Wallner (GP, Freistadt), Insp. Gruber, Pammer (neighbouring farmer), Gerti (Helga's sister, Steyr), Rudi (Friedrich's friend). Addresses: Gut Waldegg 1 (manor), 2 (caretaker), 4252 Liebenau.
- Keep added text restrained: no threats, no blood, no messages to the player.
